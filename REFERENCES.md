## Category: games (named from knowledge, not viewed for this board)
- Hue (Fiddlesticks, 2016): background colour hides same-coloured objects. Closest mechanic; stay away from its look and its framing.
- Gris (Nomada, 2018): colour returns as you progress.
- Fez (Polytron, 2012): rotating the world changes reachability.
- Celeste (2018): feel, forgiveness, feedback.
- Inside (Playdead, 2016): one grade, grain, no UI.
- Return of the Obra Dinn (Lucas Pope, 2018): a game rendered through one historic display process.
