**The swap test:** the palette, grain, lettering, shadows and giant numerals could ship almost unchanged for another retro puzzle-platformer. Those establish taste and consistency, but do not establish ownership. The primed movement predictions, overlap-dependent 18, annotated failures and generated claims would require another game to adopt this game’s mechanics and narrative structure. That is the substantial distinction.

**The removal test:** the level index can lose repeated “wherein” and “no alternative embodiment was required” clauses because the certificate already provides the full prose. The 20/22/24 callouts in the death frame can disappear without reducing actionable information. Texture and shadows could disappear without changing the rules, but their removal would weaken the shared material. The enormous cover numeral earns its space through its transition into the gameplay folio.

The highest-value fixes follow. Sizes and coordinates are **proposed targets at 1440 × 900**, not measurements of the current implementation.

1. **Explain the playable rules on the cover.** Use the empty area beginning at **(320, 300)** for two lines at **28 px / 36 px**: “Red carries you. Green kills. Blue is water.” and “Overlap all three colours to open the door.” Add the actual movement and jump bindings beside the existing lens controls, using **32 × 40 px** minimum keycaps. These are essential instructions for the first ten seconds.

2. **Identify the dial and explicitly demonstrate carriage.** Add **LENS**, **22 px**, centred above the dial at **(114, 318)**, and a signed current-step value at **30 px**, centred below it at **(114, 490)**. During the cover’s existing demonstrations, show **“On red: carried”** and **“In air: independent”** at **26 px**, each for **900 ms**. Keep gameplay inputs immediate.

3. **Strengthen foreground and preview separation.** Give the black runner a **2 px cream keyline, #F2E9D2**, on dark fields. Use **4 px** phantom strokes at **80% opacity**, with **12 px dashes / 8 px gaps**. Use **#171714** for supporting contrast on orange and **#F2E9D2** on slate. Keep primed labels at least **28 px**. The future position should remain clearly subordinate to the solid object without disappearing into its background.

4. **Give the documentary screens a reading voice.** Keep the condensed display face for titles, level names and stamps. Set index descriptions in an uncondensed face at **22 px / 28 px**, with **56 px rows**, and stop text at **x = 1190** to reserve a clean status gutter. Remove repeated boilerplate there. Set certificate claims at **20 px / 26 px** in two **636 px** columns beginning at **x = 60** and **x = 744**, retaining the complete personalised wording.

5. **Make the phone notice a useful handoff.** Retain the desktop requirement. Give the notice a maximum width of **560 CSS px**, **16 px** side margins, a **24 px / 28 px** title and **16 px / 22 px** explanation. Add **48 px-high** “Watch the demo” and “Copy desktop link” controls with a **12 px** gap. This improves the existing boundary without requiring touch platforming.

**Keep:** the primed predictive figures; distinct hatch directions; the white exit and identifier 18; the circled trajectory and ANTICIPATED wording; the numeral-to-folio transition; the sheet withdrawal; and the run-specific patent claims. Preserve the quiet space during play and the deliberate holds. Those give the player room to understand a genuinely distinctive apparatus.
