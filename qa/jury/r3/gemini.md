### 3. Swap and Removal Tests

* **Swap Test (Could these visuals ship unchanged for a different game?)**: **No.** Only the paper grain, the giant cut-paper figure numeral, and the condensed grotesque/monospace font pairing could transfer to another retro puzzle game. Every other visual element is load-bearing mechanics: the dash-dot phantom outlines (`10'`, `12'`) *are* the lens-shift preview; the leader lines and prime numerals distinguish stationary hazards (`14`) from shiftable geometry (`12`); the `20 LENS` dial and `10 CARRIED BY 12` / `10 CLEAR OF 12` indicator report the exact physics coupling rule; the `ANTICIPATED / cf. ATTEMPT 1` stamp and dotted parabola function as a persistent spatial ghost on retry; and the `BRIEF DESCRIPTION OF THE DRAWINGS` screen writes procedural patent claims from the player's actual movement history.
* **Removal Test (What could be deleted without loss?)**:
  1. **The giant tonal upper-right level title (`LENS`, `LEAP`)**: It duplicates the `SHEET X OF 10 : NAME` micro-label right above it, competes with the `ALLOWED` stamp in `FIG. 1` and `FIG. 6`, and disappears into the dark background on `FIG. 7`.
  2. **The bottom-left 4-line monospace controls reminder (`Q/E lens / A/D move / W jump / Esc sheets`) after `FIG. 1`**: Once the player has used the lens and jump on Sheet 1, keeping this text block on Sheets 2–10 adds small-type clutter below the `22`/`24` arrows.
  3. **Nothing else**: Removing the leader lines, prime marks, phantom outlines, or trajectory arc directly degrades puzzle readability.
