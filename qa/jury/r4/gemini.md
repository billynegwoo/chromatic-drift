### 3. Swap and Removal Tests

#### Swap Test (Could these visuals ship unchanged for a different game?)
- **No.** Unlike [ref-sotd-2.png](file:///private/var/folders/5z/gzftp9sd7dg_c3zsd21n8plw0000gn/T/gemini-juror-xY9ckN/ref-sotd-2.png) (a generic dark-mode sci-fi loader) or [ref-sotd-3.png](file:///private/var/folders/5z/gzftp9sd7dg_c3zsd21n8plw0000gn/T/gemini-juror-xY9ckN/ref-sotd-3.png) (a media-disc carousel), the visual language here is structurally inseparable from the mechanic. The primed phantom lines (`10'`, `12'`) only exist because the lens shifts layers in discrete detents; the USPTO cross-hatching (`12` vertical, `14` diagonal, blue horizontal) encodes the three additive channels; the `18` callout only appears on additive RGB union; and the `Brief Description of the Drawings` prose (`wherein 10 rode on 12 for two stops...`) is compiled directly from the player's lens-shift and jump history. You could not drop this skin onto a generic platformer without breaking the meaning of every callout on screen.

#### Removal Test (What could be deleted without loss?)
1. **The large embossed upper-right wordmarks (`LENS`, `LEAP`, `LIFT`)**: With `FIG. 1` / `2` / `6` on the left, the tiny `SHEET X OF 10 : NAME` header at the top right, and the full title in the `Esc` index, the 140 px embossed wordmark duplicates information and crowds the `ALLOWED` stamp on `FIG. 6` (`20.50 s`).
2. **Persistent bottom-left control text (`Q/E lens / or wheel / A/D move / W jump / Esc sheets`) after `FIG. 2`**: Once the player has completed two sheets, keeping a 5-line typewriter control block on every sheet adds left-column noise.
3. **Redundant dual-numeral leader strings (`10 / 10'` and `12 / 12'`) during active platforming (`18.17 s`–`19.50 s`)**: When the phantom preview `10'` is displaced far from `10` or when `10` is running, drawing twin white leader lines from a single floating `10 / 10'` label creates long diagonal lines that cut across platforms and doors.

### 5. Top Fixes & Keep List

#### Top Fixes (At Most 7, With Exact Values)

1. **Prevent leader-line crossings during movement and landing (`10.67 s`, `18.17 s`–`19.50 s`)**:
   - **Fix**: Split combined `10 / 10'` and `12 / 12'` dual-leader labels into independent local tags (`10` anchored `14 px` above the runner at `0°`; `10'` anchored `10 px` above the phantom box at `65%` opacity `#EDE6D3`), and clamp maximum leader-line length to `36 px` (down from `~140 px` at `19.00 s` on `FIG. 6`). Hide `10'` and `12'` while horizontal velocity `|vx| > 0`.
2. **Stop top-edge viewport clipping on vertical-shift levels (`FIG. 6` at `17.67 s`–`19.67 s`)**:
   - **Fix**: At lens `-2` on `FIG. 6`, the blue door strip (`24`) shifts upward into the top viewport border and clips to a `6 px` sliver (`y = 18 px`). Shift the entire `FIG. 6` world datum downward by `48 px` (or enforce a `40 px` top safe-margin clamp) so the full `56 px`-tall blue door strip and its phantom outline remain completely inside the sheet at lens `-2`.
3. **Add a 2-frame mechanical settle to lens steps and dial rotation (`3.83 s`, `19.83 s`)**:
   - **Fix**: Currently red (`22`) and blue (`24`) snap to their new detent in 1 frame. Add a `50 ms` (`3 frames` at 60 fps) stepped paper-slide with a `3 px` overshoot on the moving layers and a `6°` rotational bounce on the `20 LENS` dial needle (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1.35, 0.36, 1)`) so each scroll click feels like a physical detent.
4. **Resolve collision between `ALLOWED` stamp and embossed sheet title (`FIG. 6` at `20.50 s`)**:
   - **Fix**: On `FIG. 6`, the white `ALLOWED` stamp (`x = 690 px, y = 82 px`) lands directly over the embossed `LIFT` title. Anchor the `ALLOWED` stamp dynamically `24 px` left of door `18` at `y = door.top - 52 px`, and reduce the embossed title opacity from `18%` black to `10%` black (`rgba(0,0,0,0.10)`) with `top: 28 px, right: 32 px`.
5. **De-crowd `10` and `12` callouts when the runner passes the platform midpoint (`5.83 s`–`6.17 s`)**:
   - **Fix**: On `FIG. 1`, when runner `10` crosses `x = 420 px` (`5.83 s`), its leader label collides with stationary platform label `12`. Give static numeral `12` a repulsion rule: when `|x_10 - x_12| < 48 px`, slide numeral `12` horizontally by `56 px` away from `10` over `120 ms` while keeping its leader anchor fixed on the red bar.
6. **Replace `[pending]` placeholder token on the `Brief Description of the Drawings` sheet (`13.50 s`)**:
   - **Fix**: Replace `[pending]` on rows `3`–`10` with authentic 1950s patent-docket copy: `— NOT YET EXAMINED —` set in `12 px` Courier uppercase, tracked `+0.08em`, colour `#8C8575` (`55%` ink opacity on `#EDE6D3` stock).
7. **Increase legibility of the left-margin key legend and fade it after `FIG. 2`**:
   - **Fix**: Increase the bottom-left control block (`Q/E lens...`) from `9 px` (`line-height: 11 px`) to `11 px` (`line-height: 15 px`, colour `#1A1917` on ochre / `#EDE6D3` on slate and navy) on `FIG. 1` and `FIG. 2`, then reduce its opacity to `35%` on `FIG. 3`–`10` unless the player is idle for `> 4.0 s`.
