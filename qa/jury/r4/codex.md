**3. Swap and removal tests**

**Swap:** The colours, paper treatment, typography, stamps and transitions could serve another puzzle game with relatively little alteration. The complete interface could not transfer unchanged: the opposing layer directions, rider previews, overlap-generated exit and recorded claims depend on this particular mechanic. That connection gives the presentation substance beyond an attractive period treatment.

**Removal:** The tiny upper-right sheet/title line duplicates information already supplied by the large figure number and lesson title. Some repeated phantom leaders and internal outlines could disappear without weakening the prediction. The giant background lesson word contributes atmosphere more than instruction.

Removing the primed runner preview, carried/airborne distinction, failure trace or run-specific claims would materially weaken the idea. Those are essential.

**5. Top fixes**

These are proposed targets at **1440 × 900**, rather than measured defects in the implementation.

1. **Explain the lens through one direct instruction.** In FIG. 1’s empty upper playfield, place two lines at **x180, y440**, using **22 px type with 30 px leading**: “Q/E or wheel moves red and blue in opposite directions.” / “On red, you move with it. In air, you don’t.” Dismiss after the first lens change and jump, with a **160 ms fade**. The existing cover establishes colour roles more clearly than this essential relationship.

2. **Separate patent references from live instrument readings.** Reduce HUD identifiers **20, 22 and 24 to 14 px**. Keep the signed lens value at **32 px**, centred beneath the dial, and the carried/airborne message at **20 px**. The current identifiers have enough emphasis to resemble additional counters.

3. **Give previews a clear hierarchy.** Use a **2 px cream outline, #F3ECD7**, at **55% opacity** for idle alternatives and **90%** for the direction currently being requested. Keep current geometry fully opaque. Remove duplicated internal preview seams and maintain **12 px clearance** between leaders and unrelated outlines. FIG. 9 most needs this reduction.

4. **Protect door fragments against patterned terrain.** Where components overlap red hatching, use a **3 px cream outer keyline with a 2 px near-black separator, #171612**. Suppress decorative hatch marks within **6 px** of the component silhouette while retaining the terrain boundary. The exit components need to remain recognisable within FIG. 9’s red mass.

5. **Make the first failure’s meaning immediate.** Under the first **ANTICIPATED** stamp, use “CF. ATTEMPT 1 · TRY AGAIN” at **20 px**, **10 px below** the stamp. Subsequent failures can retain the shorter caption. Preserve the measured **500 ms freeze** and **433 ms reset hold**; they give the verdict and failed path time to register.

**Keep:** the enormous figure numerals; restrained sheet palettes; RGB pattern distinctions; primed platform-and-rider previews; explicit carried/airborne state; the white door numbered **18**; circled failure traces; paper transitions; and the index and Letters Patent written from the run. These carry the game’s identity.
