### 3. Swap and Removal Tests

* **Swap test (Could these visuals ship unchanged for a different game?)**: **No.** Only the paper grain and condensed grotesque display font could transfer to another mid-century title. The core visual vocabulary—primed dash-dot phantom projections (`10'`, `12'`), directional RGB hatched bars (`22`, `24`), additive overlap turning the three bars into solid white door `18`, and live leader lines tracking the separation of layers—is a direct diagram of the bilateral chromatic shift mechanic itself. Put another platformer inside this skin and the primed phantom callouts (`10'`, `12'`) and dial (`20`) have nothing to represent.
* **Removal test (What could be deleted without loss?)**:
  1. **The persistent `12` callout and leader line during normal running (`4.67 s–7.50 s`)**: Once the player is simply running flat toward an already-aligned door `18`, keeping `12` attached to the middle of the floor causes `10` and `12` to cross and tangle (`5.33 s–6.17 s`).
  2. **The giant full-height figure numeral lingering into active play (`3.00 s–3.33 s`, `9.00 s–9.33 s`, `22.00 s–22.33 s`)**: Once the platforms and callouts appear, the giant numeral collides with platform `12` and callout `10'` before shrinking to the top-left HUD at `3.50 s` / `9.50 s` / `22.50 s`. Shrinking it as part of the intro build removes the overlap without losing the title-sequence punch.

### 4. Verdict Scores

#### Awwwards Rubric (1–10)

* **Design: 8.1 / 10** — *Every UI element (HUD dial `20`, phantom previews `10'`/`12'`, hit markers, level select) is natively drafted as part of a 1950s patent sheet and Saul Bass cut-paper sequence, held back only by callout leader-line collisions (`1.83 s`, `5.50 s`, `16.00 s`) and poor red-on-burgundy contrast on `FIG. 7` (`21.83 s`).*
  * **What stops a veteran juror:** The moment you realize the dashed technical drawing lines (`10'`, `12'`) and numbered leader lines (`10`, `12`, `18`) are not decorative static illustrations—they are the live collision and predictive HUD of an active platformer.
* **Usability: 7.5 / 10** — *The cover sheet's self-running comparative diagram (`ON RED: CARRIED` vs. `IN THE AIR: NOT CARRIED`, `0.50 s–2.17 s`) and the primed phantom outlines (`10'`, `12'`) make a tricky bilateral-shift rule legible before committing, though red-on-burgundy low contrast in `FIG. 7` (`21.83 s`) and black-on-dark-green callout `10'` (`22.00 s`) hurt instant readability.*
* **Creativity: 8.4 / 10** — *Fusing a 1950s USPTO patent drawing with a Saul Bass cut-paper title sequence and turning player deaths into persistent "prior art" (`ANTICIPATED / CF. ATTEMPT 1` at `12.17 s`) is an unforgettable, unified formal conceit.*
  * **What stops a veteran juror:** Dying at `11.83 s` and watching your failed jump immediately become a dashed patent figure annotation (`ANTICIPATED / CF. ATTEMPT 1`) that stays printed on the sheet during your next attempt (`12.50 s–13.00 s`), followed by the `Esc` menu (`13.50 s`) writing live legal prose about your exact mid-air move.
* **Content: 8.2 / 10** — *Zero lorem ipsum or generic game-jam HUD strings; every label, stamp (`ALLOWED`, `ANTICIPATED`), and dynamically generated figure description (`wherein 10 rode on 12 for two stops; no alternative embodiment was required`) commits 100% to patent-prosecution language.*
  * **What stops a veteran juror:** Opening the level select at `13.50 s` and seeing row 2 (`LEAP`) already updated with a bespoke patent-specification clause describing the exact mid-air ledge slide performed three seconds earlier (`shows the far ledge slid under the runner in mid-air`).
* **Craft in Motion: 7.8 / 10** — *Against the reference sheets—where `ref-sotd-2.png` stalls on a static dark loader for 34 frames and `ref-sotd-1.png` leans on stock glitch cuts—every input and state transition here (`2.67 s` 3-step cut-in, `7.67 s` `433 ms` stamp hold, `8.17 s` paper peel, `13.50 s` row cascade) moves like cut card stock and ink stamps rather than default CSS easing.*

### 5. Top Fixes & Keep List

#### Top Fixes (Exact Values)

1. **Replace the `FIG. 7` burgundy background to restore red-channel contrast (`21.50 s–23.17 s`)**:
   * **Issue**: The deep burgundy sheet (`#6E1E19`) reduces the contrast ratio of the red platform (`#C82822`) and red dash-dot phantom outlines (`12'`) to `< 1.6:1`, making the red door piece and phantom lines nearly invisible.
   * **Fix**: Shift the `FIG. 7` sheet background to a muted olive-drab (`#4A5438`) or warm charcoal (`#2B2D2F`) so red (`#D43228`), green (`#2E8B47`), and blue (`#2C62B8`) all maintain at least a `3.2:1` luminance or chromatic separation against the paper field.
2. **Eliminate the HUD transition overlap between the `FIG.` numeral and `LENS` label (`16.00 s` / `22.50 s`)**:
   * **Issue**: When the giant figure numeral shrinks to the top-left HUD (`16.00 s` in `FIG. 6`, `22.50 s` in `FIG. 7`), its bottom edge sits at `y = 148 px` (on a `1080p` canvas), colliding for `80–160 ms` with the `LENS` dial header at `y = 142 px`.
   * **Fix**: Cap the shrunken `FIG.` numeral height at `76 px` (top at `y = 58 px`, bottom at `y = 134 px`) and push the `LENS` label and dial (`20`) down `18 px` so `LENS` starts at `y = 158 px` (`24 px` clear vertical gap).
3. **Shrink the giant intro numeral `333 ms` earlier, before callouts stamp in (`3.00 s`, `9.00 s`, `22.00 s`)**:
   * **Issue**: On `FIG. 2` (`9.00 s–9.33 s`) and `FIG. 7` (`22.00 s–22.33 s`), the full-height cream numeral remains behind the level geometry for `500 ms` after the platforms and callouts appear, causing `10'` and platform `12` to overlap the numeral stroke.
   * **Fix**: Trigger the giant numeral scale-down transition (`250 ms` stepped cut-paper collapse) at step 2 of the sheet build (`2.83 s`, `8.83 s`, `21.83 s`) so the numeral is already in its top-left HUD position before the callout layer (`10`, `10'`, `12`, `12'`) stamps in at `3.00 s` / `9.00 s` / `22.00 s`.
4. **Add a cream paper halo/knockout behind callout numerals over dark/hatched geometry (`10.83 s`, `22.00 s`)**:
   * **Issue**: On `FIG. 7` (`22.00 s–22.33 s`), black callout `10'` lands directly on top of the dark green/black-hatched spike rack (`#1D3324`), and on `FIG. 2` (`10.83 s–11.50 s`), cream callout `12'` sits inside the red-and-black vertical hatching of platform `12`.
   * **Fix**: Give every callout numeral (`10`, `10'`, `12`, `12'`, `14`, `18`) a tight rectangular paper-coloured knockout backing (`padding: 2px 4px`, matching the active sheet background colour or `#EFEAD8` cream when over geometry) so hatching lines never pass through numeral glyphs.
5. **Offset the cover-sheet `12'` callout above the left edge or move the rule caption down `16 px` (`1.83 s–2.00 s`)**:
   * **Issue**: During the cover sheet's `IN THE AIR: NOT CARRIED` demonstration (`1.83 s–2.00 s`), the left-hand `12'` callout and its leader line overlap directly on top of the word `IN`.
   * **Fix**: Move the cover caption line (`ON RED: CARRIED` / `IN THE AIR: NOT CARRIED`) down `16 px` (from `y = 758 px` to `y = 774 px` on `1080p`) and anchor the left `12'` callout vertically centered on the left edge of the phantom bar (`y = 685 px`, `24 px` left of the bar).
6. **Clamp the door `18` leader-line length and angle so it cannot collide with the corner peel (`20.50 s–21.17 s`)**:
   * **Issue**: On `FIG. 6` (`20.67 s–21.17 s`), because the aligned door sits high on the right (`x = 84%`, `y = 30%`), the leader line for `18` shoots up and right for `140 px` to the top-right corner of the viewport, where the page-curl triangle clips right through the numeral `18`.
   * **Fix**: Constrain callout `18`'s leader line to `36 px` length at a `-45°` (down-right) or `+135°` (up-left) angle whenever the door is in the upper half of the viewport (`y < 50%`), keeping `18` at least `120 px` clear of the top-right corner curl zone.
7. **Fade or de-emphasize static floor callout `12` once the lens is locked on an aligned door (`4.67 s–7.50 s`)**:
   * **Issue**: As runner `10` runs across `FIG. 1` (`5.33 s–6.17 s`), the moving callout `10` passes straight through the stationary callout `12`, briefly reading as `1012` / `1210` with crossed leader lines.
   * **Fix**: When `|x(10) - x(12)| < 48 px`, smoothly flip `10`'s leader line to `-60°` (up-left, `28 px` tall) while keeping `12` at `+45°` or below the top edge of the bar so their bounding boxes stay separated by at least `14 px`.

#### Keep List (What Must Not Be Lost)

* **The self-running cover-sheet comparative diagram (`0.50 s–2.17 s`)**: Teaching `ON RED: CARRIED` vs. `IN THE AIR: NOT CARRIED` before the player presses `Space` is a masterclass in wordless-plus-three-word onboarding.
* **Primed dash-dot phantom outlines (`10'`, `12'`)**: Showing both where the platform will land (`12'`) and whether the runner will move with it (`10'`) makes planning multi-step lens shifts fair and tactile.
* **The `ANTICIPATED / CF. ATTEMPT 1` death freeze (`533 ms` at `12.17 s`) and persistent dashed trajectory arc (`11.83 s–13.00 s`)**: Turning player failure into an annotated patent prior-art figure that persists onto the next attempt is the single best moment in the game.
* **The dynamic patent-claim prose in the `Esc` "Brief Description of the Drawings" sheet (`13.50 s–15.00 s`)**: Recording how the player actually solved or attempted each sheet in dry USPTO legal syntax (`wherein 10 rode on 12 for two stops; no alternative embodiment was required`) makes the theme structural.
* **The 3-beat cut-paper level build (`2.67 s–3.00 s`), `433 ms` `ALLOWED` stamp hold (`7.67 s`), and angled sheet pull-off (`8.17 s–8.50 s`)**: The tactile pacing and material honesty of these transitions give the game its signature rhythm.
